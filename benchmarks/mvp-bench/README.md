- Harnesses: Riff, GSD, gstack
- Briefs: salon booking, review insights, quote management
- Runs: three per harness, nine total
- Success: no critical failure, at least 90% acceptance, persistence, research-to-decision evidence
- Reporting: raw results and medians; no invented weighted score

The comparison table will be published only after the nine human runs. Until then, no superiority
claim is made.
